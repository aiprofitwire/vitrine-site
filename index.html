Page to video

A long page becomes a smooth video.

Vitrine scrolls your capture from top to bottom as an MP4, in 16:9, 1:1, 4:5 or 9:16. Made from your capture, in your framed look, on your computer.

Hide ads

One switch, and the ads are gone.

Turn on Hide ads and the ads, empty ad boxes and pop-overs stay out of the shot. Nothing is blocked on the page itself.

Brand match · Pro

Or let the page pick the colours.

Brand match reads the site’s own colours and builds a backdrop that fits. Prefer one look everywhere? Pick a backdrop like Vermilion or Ink and save it as your style.

The Studio

Point at what matters.

Arrows, boxes, highlights, labels, numbered steps and blur. Crop it, then export to PNG, JPG or PDF, or copy it straight to your clipboard.

Carousels

One capture, a whole carousel.

Numbered 4:5 slides, each one cut between blocks of text. Export a PDF for LinkedIn or PNG slides for Instagram.

Side by side

Put 2 to 4 captures on one image: pricing pages, before and after, A/B tests.

Step-by-step guides

Each capture becomes a numbered step. Add a note to each, then export a clean PDF or Markdown.
