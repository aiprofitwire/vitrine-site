How it works

One shortcut, start to finish.

  1. ⌥⇧S

    Windows: Alt + Shift + S

    1Press the shortcut

    Or click the Vitrine icon. Four shortcuts cover the full page, the visible area, a region and an element.

  2. 2Vitrine does the scrolling

    It scrolls the page for you, loads the lazy images, shows sticky headers once and leaves pop-ups and ads out.

  3. 3Your look is already on

    The Studio opens with your framed look applied. Copy it, export it, or turn it into a video.

Questions

Good questions.

Do I need an account?

No. Not for the free version, not for Pro. Pro is a licence key you paste once, and it works offline.

Is the free version really free?

Yes. All four capture modes, markup, Instant Copy and export are free for good. Everything free today stays free in every 1.x update.

Does it handle long pages?

Yes. Vitrine scrolls the whole page for you, shows sticky headers once and keeps very long pages sharp, even pages that scroll inside a box, like inboxes and dashboards. Press Esc on a very long page to stop and keep what you have.

Where are my screenshots stored?

In a private library in your browser, on your computer. Back it all up as one zip whenever you like.

What does Vitrine send over the internet?

Nothing, except a one-time licence check when you activate Pro.

Which browser does it need?

Google Chrome on your computer.

Is it in French?

Yes. Vitrine speaks English and French, and follows your Chrome language.

What if Pro isn’t right for me?

Email us within 14 days of your purchase and we’ll refund you in full.

How do I reach you?

Write to support@metadatamarketer.com. A real person replies within 3 business days.
